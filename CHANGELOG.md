# Wanderer Maps 1.3.54

### New
- Guided route, time or money: the fastest, balanced (a flight's fare counted as the time your level takes to earn it) or the cheapest. The fares are learned at each flight master; a flight you cannot pay is left out.
- The way adapts to where you stand: while you follow a route, a clearly better way from your position (a portal, another flight, a detour) replaces the one followed, said once.

### Fixed
- A walk through a flight master or a dock passed on the way is no longer split into two steps.
- With a quest pinned, the quests to hand in stay plain to see on the Atlas instead of fading with the others.
- Your corpse on the Atlas: the game's skull, above the other marks, easy to find on any map (it drew a strip of small icons).
- A route of more than nine steps: the Atlas's strip follows the step you are on, no longer stuck on the first nine.
- A quest with more than seven rewards on the Atlas: all of them shown, a little smaller.
- More quests on a map than the Atlas's panel holds: its last line counts the others and opens the journal.
- Shift and a click on a quest of the Atlas (its panel, its journal, its marks on the map) puts its link in the chat, as in the game's log.
- The Atlas journal shows failed quests in red and the quest tags; sharing or abandoning keeps the game log's choice; the tracking limit is said.
- /watlas opens the atlas.
- The arrow catches the mouse only on its sign and its name, and stays put in a fight.
- A damaged save no longer breaks the arrow: broken notes and steps are left out.

### Changed
- Your quests' zones on the map: one zone for each creature or object asked, with its own outline; under the mouse, the zone names what is there to kill or take.
- The Atlas's zoom out button shows a minus, no longer an arrow.
- The arrow stays clear near the place it leads to, and readable when the place is in another zone.
- On the minimap, the step followed stays bright even at the edge.
- On the Atlas, your group's discs are larger; the button to the map around is an up arrow, back to the map's overview a back arrow; Rename shows a pencil.
- The Atlas resizes from the game's usual corner and remembers its place; its search shows places before your notes (three at most) and counts the others; your own position shows when the mouse is away.
- Steps and notes on the minimap name themselves under the mouse.
- Every place has the same clicks: Shift a map link, Ctrl a step of the route (route window, services, atlas map).
- Atlas: Ctrl+click adds a step, reward icons link with Shift, a search with no match says so.
- Clearer verbs: "Clear" the route, "Delete" a note or a kept route.
