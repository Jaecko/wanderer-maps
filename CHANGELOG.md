# Wanderer Maps 1.2.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (needs Wanderer 1.4 or later; Wanderer Quests optional). Everything is drawn by the game's own map engine: its zoom, pins and clicks stay as they are.

### New
- Notes on the world map: Alt+click opens a small card where you clicked, a name and one of eight signs (place, star, treasure, herbs, ore, danger, meeting, question); Enter saves, Shift+Enter saves and follows, Ctrl+Enter puts it on the route.
- A route of steps, from your notes or from Wanderer Quests' places ("Go there", "Add to the route", a whole "Quest route"); a key of yours leaves a note where you stand.
- An arrow on the screen toward the step followed, placed where you like: gold on course, amber when the point is behind you, with the distance, the time of arrival and the whole route's time. Near a step, the next one is followed; the last one ends the route.
- A step of Wanderer Quests says what to do there, opens its quest in one click and ticks itself once the quest is taken or handed in.
- When no step is followed, the arrow follows the game's own map pin: yours, a Wanderer Quests place, a trainer.
- The guided way: to a step, the quickest way in time through your feet, the flight paths you have found, the boats, zeppelins and tram of the original world, and your hearthstone if you wish (off by default, its wait counted while it cools down); the arrow leads leg by leg.
- The game's floating marker in the world on the point followed, and the route on the minimap: a numbered pin for each step, a dotted line, your notes within a radius you choose.
- A window with two tabs, Route and Notes: distances kept fresh, a "..." on each row, the nearest inn, flight master, mailbox and trainer you know, one click to follow.
- Routes kept under a name (a round of herbs, of mailboxes), followed again later, renamed or removed.
- After a death, the arrow points to your corpse while you are a ghost, gone at resurrection.
- A Notes button on the world map shows or hides your notes by sign, or all at once (the minimap follows); on a continent's map they are small dots, grouped by zone when many.
- The Notes tab sorts your notes by last use and removes every note of a sign or of a zone at once, asked first. Shift+click a note or a step: a map link in your chat box, never sent.
- The continents in one piece: each zone's own land drawn where it lies on its continent's map, as sharp as on its own map. The land you have not explored yet shows softened, on zones and continents.
- Beside the zone's name in the map's bar: its levels in the color of their difficulty for you, the fishing skill its waters ask, and how many areas you have explored.
- In flight, the destination's name over a slim line from take off to landing: a dot for each stop, yours moving along, the time left. Times learned from your own flights, guessed from the distance before (marked ~), shown in the flight master's map too.
- Optional, off by default: small marks for the inns, mailboxes, banks, auction houses and class trainers you have visited and your side's flight masters; a quiet notice for a flight master of your side you have not found yet.
- With TomTom or a guide's own arrow (RestedXP, Zygor), Maps' arrow steps aside; with TomTom it never sets the game's marker.
- Opened from Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wmaps. Every setting in Wanderer's options, one checkbox turns off everything outside the window.
