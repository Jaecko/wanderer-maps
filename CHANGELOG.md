# Wanderer Maps 1.3.55

### Changed
- On the game's map in a fight, a short line says your notes and your route come back once it ends.
- The Atlas trail of maps shortens from the left when the window is narrow, the cut start a click away.
- The Atlas says where you look as a trail of maps (the world, the continent, the zone), each one a click away, instead of a single arrow up.
- Flight progress shows while your interface has faded away, and leaves as it comes back: a thin line across the screen, a dot for each stop with its name above it, yours moving along, no frame around it, at the foot of the screen.
- On long flights each stop stands where its time puts it: stretches you flew before by their real time, the others by their length at the pace of the known ones (from the flight map when a stop is not placed in the world), and the stops passed on the way place the next ones again.
- With the game's look (a choice under Wanderer's Appearance): the Atlas side panel, its lists and journal headings drawn with the game's own parts, its lit and chosen rows, its dividers and colors. Needs Wanderer 1.4.89.

### Fixed
- The Atlas search finds names with capitals and accents ("Elwynn", "Forêt"), notes too.
- The route strip under the Atlas keeps its steps within the window on a small screen.
- With a controller, the card of a quest, objective, giver or notes pin goes, adds a step and writes a note again.
