# Wanderer Maps

A route with steps, notes on the world map, an arrow on the screen. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- A route with steps: from your notes, from where you stand (a key), from the places of [Wanderer Quests](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-quests) ("Go there", "Add to the route", a whole "Quest route" through a place's quests). Each step says what to do there (take a quest, hand it in), opens its quest in one click and ticks itself once done. An arrow follows the step, gold on course and amber when it is behind you, with the distance, the time of arrival ("~2 min") and the step's number with the whole route's time; reached, the next one; the last one ends the route. It points as the crow flies, and steps aside when TomTom or a guide shows its own arrow.
- Routes kept: the route followed kept under a name (a round of herbs, of mailboxes), followed again later, renamed or removed.
- After a death, an arrow to your corpse while you are a ghost (an option, never with TomTom), gone at resurrection.
- The guided way: to a step, the cheapest way in time through your feet, the flight paths you have found, the boats, zeppelins and tram of the original world, your hearthstone (an option of each character, its wait counted while it cools down); followed leg by leg.
- On a flight, the destination named over a slim line from take off to landing: a dot for each stop, yours moving along, the time left. Times learned from your own flights, guessed from the distance before (marked ~); the flight map's tooltips say them too.
- Notes on the world map: Alt+click opens a small card where you click: a name and one of eight signs (a star, a treasure, herbs, ore, danger...), saved, followed or put on the route. Notes show on the minimap too, within a radius you choose. A button on the world map shows or hides them by sign, or all at once (the minimap follows); on a continent's map they are small dots, grouped by zone when many. Shift+click: a map link in the chat with the note's name and zone. The Notes tab sorts them by last use and removes all notes of a sign or of a zone at once, asked first.
- The game's own marker in the world on the point followed; the route on the minimap.
- The continents in one piece: every zone's own land drawn where it lies on its continent's map, as sharp as on its own map; the land you have not explored yet softened, on the zones' maps too. Drawn by the map's own engine, from the game's own pieces.
- Beside the zone's name: its levels in the color of their difficulty for you, the fishing skill its waters ask, how many areas you have explored. Optional marks for the town services you have visited and a notice for a flight master not found yet.
- Opened from Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wmaps. Settings in Wanderer's options.
